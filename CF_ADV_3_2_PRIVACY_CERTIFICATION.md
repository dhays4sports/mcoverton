# CF-ADV-3.2 — Privacy, Consent & Access Certification

Status: **PASS**

Certified controls: anonymous preview; independent report-save, contact, and SMS consent; private validated policy files; hashed opaque 30-day return tokens; no-store customer reports; authenticated producer mutations; immutable report history; internal-note separation; expiry purging; and explicit token-authenticated deletion of the journey plus its private policy files.

Saving a report does not grant contact permission. Contact permission does not grant SMS permission. No response, decision, or report action authorizes binding.
