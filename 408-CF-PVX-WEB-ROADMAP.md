## Migration boundary

408FARMERS owns acquisition, campaign context, Dylan relationship positioning, and direct contact choices. CoverageFit remains the single owner of PVX journey state, discovery, Snapshot, reports, Home Profile, Current Policy Review, recommendations, score, resume, and producer projections.

New web traffic uses a top-level POST bootstrap and an HttpOnly resume credential. No iframe, floating widget, duplicated assessment, or PII-bearing continuation URL is permitted. Existing SMS handoffs, historical reports, legacy consultations, Life, Local, renter, and direct-contact behavior remain compatible.
