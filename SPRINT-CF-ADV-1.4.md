# CF-ADV-1.4 — “Why Are We Here?” Opening

Release: CoverageFit 3.20.75
Program: CF-ADV-1 — Personalized Advisory Conversation
Status: Complete

## Goal

Start the substantive CoverageFit review with customer motivation and tradeoff preference instead of insurance terminology. The opening must preserve zero-repeat handoff behavior and remain outside Protection Score.

## Customer experience

After the home is confirmed, CoverageFit asks only two advisory questions before the coverage review begins:

1. **What’s bringing you here today?**
   - My insurance is getting expensive
   - I’m comparing options
   - I’m buying a home
   - My renewal is coming up
   - I’m unhappy with my current company
   - I want to make sure I’m properly covered
   - I recently had a life change
   - Something else

2. **What matters most in this review?**
   - Keep my cost down
   - Find the right balance
   - Protect myself as strongly as practical
   - I’m not sure yet

If a trusted 408FARMERS handoff already contains the reason (or a bounded Home review goal), CoverageFit shows that value as connected and does not ask the question again unless the customer selects **Change**. The same behavior is supported for a recognized future priority field.

## Data behavior

The opening writes only to the existing CF-ADV-1.1 `discoveryProfile`:

- `reasonForReview`
- `primaryPriority`
- one customer statement preserving the reason wording

Every record carries `source` and `evidenceRefs`. Direct answers use `coveragefit_assessment`; inherited intake values use `408farmers_handoff`.

The opening does not create a parallel customer store. Continuity saves only a resumable UI/state copy and the final report persists the normalized `discoveryProfile`.

## Downstream behavior

- The selected reason is exposed through `activeReviewReason()` so the established Home review-reason contextual ordering layer can use it. That layer is already contractually non-scoring.
- The selected priority is consumed by CF-ADV-1.2 and may deterministically create `tradeoffPreference.price`, `.balanced`, or `.protection`. `unsure` intentionally creates no tradeoff preference signal.
- CF-ADV-1.3 may use an active tradeoff signal as recommendation rationale only for a recommendation topic already generated by established logic.

## Zero-repeat behavior

Trusted connected motivation is displayed, not silently hidden. The customer sees what CoverageFit carried forward and may correct it. The product therefore avoids repeating a question while keeping the advisory record auditable.

## Score boundary

CF-ADV-1.4 does not alter:

- Protection Score implementation
- question weights
- answer impacts
- evidence-quality scoring
- recommendation generation
- recommendation ranking
- carrier eligibility, pricing, underwriting, or binding behavior

## Continuity

`advisoryOpening` is saved inside the existing assessment continuity draft. A completed opening resumes directly into the substantive review. An incomplete opening resumes at the opening. Retaking the review resets opening selections while preserving trusted connected intake facts when available.

## Accessibility/mobile

- Native radio controls remain semantically present.
- Connected values are visible text, not color-only state.
- Focus-visible treatment is provided for choice cards.
- Touch targets and one-column mobile layout are preserved.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative movement.
- Completion status uses a polite live region.

## Definition of done

A customer cannot enter the substantive review until CoverageFit has a reason and a primary priority, where `unsure` is a valid explicit priority state. Trusted handoff facts are not re-asked by default. All output is evidence-backed and stays outside Protection Score.
